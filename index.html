        Contests and candidates

        Each contest is labeled with the jurisdiction that votes on it. District contests appear only for voters who live in that district, so the exact ballot depends on the street address. Candidate positions are the candidates' own words or campaign claims; public-record items come from independent sources; "not found" means the research found nothing, not that nothing exists.

        Ballot measures

        Yes/No meanings and fiscal notes come from official materials (Secretary of State pamphlet, Legislative Council, JLBC, Maricopa County). Supporters' and opponents' arguments are in the pamphlet and are not official analysis.

        Judicial retention

        Three different things. Leading in reported results means ahead in the votes counted so far. Projected winner is a call by a named news organization (such as AP). Certified is the official result after canvass. "Precincts reporting" is not the share of votes counted. Any "expected vote" percentage is the provider's estimate and is labeled as such; where none exists, the remaining total is unknown.

            Figures under review

            When a source shows an unexplained decrease, a different number for the same period, a very large jump, or a figure above registered voters, the published figure stays on screen marked "under review" and both numbers are kept here. Every hourly run re-reads the source; a change seen on two consecutive hourly reads is adopted and logged as a correction. Figures above registration are never adopted automatically.
